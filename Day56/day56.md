# Day 56 – CareerCopilot AI: Final Project & Deployment

## 🚀 Project Overview

For Day 56 of the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge, I completed and deployed **CareerCopilot AI**, an AI-powered career preparation platform designed to help students prepare for technical interviews and track their career learning progress.

The application uses AI to generate interview questions, evaluate answers, provide feedback, and recommend topics for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of CareerCopilot AI is to provide students with an interactive platform where they can:

- Practice job-specific interviews
- Receive AI-generated interview questions
- Get feedback on their answers
- Receive a readiness score
- Identify areas that need improvement
- Track previous interview performance
- Follow a career learning roadmap

---

## ✨ Main Features

### 1. Role Selection

Users can select a target career role such as:

- AI/ML Engineer Intern
- Data Analyst
- Other supported career roles

The selected role is used to generate relevant interview questions.

### 2. AI-Powered Interview

The application generates interview questions using the Gemini API.

The interview includes different types of questions, including:

- Introduction questions
- Technical questions
- Problem-solving questions
- Scenario-based questions
- Behavioral questions

### 3. AI Answer Evaluation

After completing the interview, the application evaluates the candidate's answers and generates:

- Final readiness score
- Overall feedback
- Recommended topics to study

### 4. Interview Dashboard

The dashboard displays:

- Readiness score
- Learning progress
- Number of completed milestones
- Interview history
- Previous interview scores
- Selected career role

### 5. Learning Roadmap

The roadmap helps users track their progress toward their target career role.
