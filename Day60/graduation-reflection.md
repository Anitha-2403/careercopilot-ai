# Day 60 — Graduation Reflection

Sixty days ago this started as a challenge to learn AI fundamentals and prompt engineering. It ends with a real, deployed application: CareerCopilot AI, a career-preparation platform with a React frontend on Netlify and an Express backend on Render, covering ten working sections — Dashboard, Coding Practice, Aptitude Hub, Interview Prep, Resume Master, Company Prep, Study Planner, Achievements, Analytics, and Settings.

The 10-day capstone brought the full journey together: scoping the project, building the client/server architecture, deploying across two platforms, and debugging a real production CORS issue between the Netlify frontend and the Render backend on Day 9. Day 10 closed out the gaps that don't show up in a demo but matter to anyone reading the repo cold — a missing LICENSE, an empty README, missing SEO and social metadata — before tagging and shipping v1.0.0.

What stands out isn't a single feature. It's the discipline: holding scope for ten days, tracking known gaps instead of hiding them, and finishing the unglamorous work before calling it done. That's the actual skill this challenge built. CareerCopilot AI is the proof of it — a shipped, working, honestly-documented project.
