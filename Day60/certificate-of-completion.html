AB Talks · 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

Sixty days of building, one shipped application at the end

How each phase of the challenge fed directly into designing, building, debugging, and deploying CareerCopilot AI.

Weeks 1–2

AI Foundations & Prompting

Learned how to work with Claude effectively — clear prompts, iterative refinement, and reasoning through problems step by step. This became the working method for the entire challenge, capstone included.

Weeks 3–4

Research & Product Thinking

Practiced scoping problems before building — defining what a project should actually do before writing code. Directly shaped how CareerCopilot AI's feature set was scoped without drift.

Weeks 5–6

Automation & Applied AI Projects

Built increasingly complex AI-assisted projects, developing the habit of pairing with Claude on real implementation work rather than just conversation.

Weeks 7–8

Systems & Agentic Workflows

Explored knowledge systems and multi-step agent workflows — groundwork for architecting a real client/server application with a defined structure rather than an ad hoc script.

Days 1–8

Capstone: Build

Ten sections built out — Dashboard, Coding Practice, Aptitude Hub, Interview Prep, Resume Master, Company Prep, Study Planner, Achievements, Analytics, Settings — on a React frontend and Express backend.

Day 9

Capstone: Production Deployment

Deployed frontend to Netlify and backend to Render. Diagnosed and fixed a real CORS misconfiguration between the two — the kind of bug only production surfaces.

Day 10

Capstone: Final Review & Polish

Full five-lens review — Engineer, PM, UX, Recruiter, and Open Source Maintainer. Closed out documentation, project metadata, and production-readiness gaps before tagging v1.0.0.

Day 60 — Capstone Result

CareerCopilot AI, v1.0.0

A deployed career-preparation web application — the outcome that validates sixty days of learning, not the point of it.
